FAQ

Common questions

Can't find what you're looking for? Get in touch.

Do you work on commercial and residential projects?

Yes. We carry out all types of renovation and maintenance for homes, restaurants, shops and offices across London.

Can you work outside opening hours?

Yes. For restaurants and businesses we work early mornings, late evenings and on closing days so trading is not interrupted.

Which areas of London do you cover?

Central, West and South West London, including Marylebone, Soho, Chelsea, Notting Hill, Fulham, Clapham, Wimbledon, Kingston, Sutton and Worcester Park.

How do I get a price?
