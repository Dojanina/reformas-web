2. What personal data we collect

We only collect the information you choose to give us when you contact us:

  • Quote form: your name, phone number, email (optional), postcode, type of property and job description.
  • WhatsApp, phone or email: your contact details, messages, and any photos you send of the job.
  • When we carry out work: the address of the property, access arrangements, quotes, invoices and payment records.

We do not collect special category data (such as health information) and we ask you not to send it to us.

4. Who we share it with

We only share your data with trusted providers who help us run the business, and only as much as they need:

  • FormSubmit — delivers the website quote form to our inbox. It may process data outside the UK (in the USA); such transfers rely on appropriate safeguards such as standard contractual clauses.
  • Our email provider and website host — store our emails and run this website.
  • Instagram and WhatsApp (Meta) — if you choose to message us on WhatsApp or Instagram, Meta's own privacy policy also applies.
  • Our accountant — for invoices and tax records.
  • Subcontractors — only when needed to complete your job (for example, an electrician), and only the details required.

We may also share data if required by law.

5. How long we keep it

  • Enquiries that do not lead to work: deleted within 12 months.
  • Customer and job records, quotes and invoices: kept for 6 years after the job, as required for tax and accounting purposes, then deleted.
  • Photos and testimonials: kept until you withdraw your consent.

6. How we protect it

Your information is kept on password-protected devices and accounts, and is only accessed by people who need it to deal with your job. This website is served over a secure (HTTPS) connection.

7. Your rights

Under UK data protection law you have the right to:

  • ask for a copy of the personal data we hold about you;
  • ask us to correct anything that is wrong or incomplete;
  • ask us to delete your data, where we are not legally required to keep it;
  • object to, or ask us to restrict, how we use your data;
  • withdraw consent at any time, for example for a published photo or testimonial;
  • ask for your data in a portable format.

To use any of these rights, email info@handymattlondon.com. We will reply within one month.

If you are unhappy with how we have handled your data, you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO) at ico.org.uk or on 0303 123 1113. We would appreciate the chance to sort it out with you first.

8. Cookies

This website does not use cookies and does not use analytics, tracking or advertising tools. Our fonts and images are hosted on our own server, so no data is sent to Google or other third parties just by visiting the site. That is why you do not see a cookie banner.

If you click a link to a third-party website (for example WhatsApp, Instagram or one of our clients' websites), that website may use its own cookies under its own policy.

If we add analytics or advertising tools in the future, we will update this policy and ask for your consent before setting any non-essential cookies.

9. Photos of our work

Photos on this website show work we have carried out. We never publish a customer's name, address or anything that identifies a property without their permission, and we remove any photo on request.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The latest version will always be on this page, with the date at the top.
